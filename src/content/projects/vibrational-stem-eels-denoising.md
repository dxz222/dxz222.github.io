## Overview

<p class="note"><strong>Context:</strong> Undergraduate thesis research at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Wu Zhou.</p>

We use machine-learning methods designed for three-dimensional spectrum-image data to denoise vibrational STEM-EELS measurements.

This project is part of the DeepSTEM research series. Further technical details and results will be shared after publication.
